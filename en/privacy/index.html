Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

NoteInk is a note-taking app for iPad. The controller is Yanick Sommerhalder, Switzerland. Contact: support@noteink.ch.

The app

  • No data collection: the app collects no personal data, sends nothing to the developer or third parties, and contains no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your notes are stored on your device. With “iCloud sync” turned on, they are synced between your devices through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit, the private database of your Apple Account). The developer has no access to them; Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Camera and Photos: the camera is used only when you take a photo or scan a document. Only what you save yourself is saved to Photos.
  • Handwriting recognition for search runs entirely on the device.
  • Purchases and App Store: downloads and the unlock are handled by Apple. The developer receives only aggregated sales figures from Apple and, if you allowed it in your iPad's settings, anonymous crash reports.

This website

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, no ads and no third-party content.
  • It is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, including for security purposes. GitHub's privacy statement applies.

Email

If you write to support, your address and message are used only to reply to you. The mailbox is hosted by Infomaniak in Switzerland.

Your rights

You can ask at any time what data the developer holds about you and have it corrected or deleted. In practice this only concerns emails to support. Write to support@noteink.ch.
