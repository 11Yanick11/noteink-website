What NoteInk can do

Write like on paper

  • Ballpoint, felt pen, brush, pencil and highlighter – pressure and tilt included
  • Ink appears instantly under the tip, even on full pages
  • Erase whole strokes, parts of strokes or just an area

Shapes that snap

  • Draw and hold: lines, arrows, circles, rectangles and polygons become perfect shapes
  • Square roots, sum signs, curly brackets, double underlines and dimension lines are tidied up automatically
  • Shapes snap to corners and lines; guides help you line things up
  • Scribble over something to delete it, circle it to select it

Everything on the page

  • Text boxes, sticky notes, photos, camera and document scans
  • Fold-out pages next to any page for extra space
  • Tape to hide answers – tap to reveal, perfect for learning
  • Lasso to move, scale, rotate, recolor or copy anything

PDF, done right

  • Import PDFs, write on them, fill in forms, follow links and select text
  • Export as PDF or picture, share notebooks, print up to 9 pages per sheet

Find it again

  • Search your own handwriting, text boxes and PDFs – recognized on your iPad
  • Library with folders, tags, favorites and a trash
  • Tabs for open notebooks and a page overview to reorder pages

Present

  • Laser pointer with dot or trail
  • On an external screen or AirPlay, only your pages and the laser are shown

Your notes, your devices

  • iCloud sync across your iPads
  • No account, no ads, no tracking – your notes stay with you
  • Light and dark mode, 15 color schemes and accent colors

Fair price

NoteInk is free for 5 notebooks, PDFs or documents. Need more? Unlock NoteInk Unlimited once and keep it forever – no subscription. With Family Sharing, your whole family can use it.
